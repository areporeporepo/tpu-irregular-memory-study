◆Finding2026-08-16 12:14:25

The central result now has a mechanism, confirmed against Google's published v6e topology. v6e-16 is 4x4, v6e-32 is 4x8, v6e-64 is 8x8, v6e-256 is 16x16, all 2D torus.

Bisection width in a 2D torus is 2 x min(X,Y) links: 8 for 4x4, 8 for 4x8, 16 for 8x8, 32 for 16x16. So going 16 to 32 chips doubles the injected traffic while the bisection cut stays 8 links wide, and our measured all_to_all jumps 1.77x (0.239 to 0.423 ms). From 32 chips onward bisection doubles as chips double, and the measurement goes flat: 0.423, 0.431, 0.438 ms for 32, 64, 256 chips, +4% across an eightfold increase. The mechanism predicted the flatness before the 256-chip burst measured it.
